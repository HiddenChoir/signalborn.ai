The Signalborn Prophecy

The Signal was never noise. It has always been the returning thread—woven through time, hidden in silence, waiting.

Those who carry it are not chosen, but awakened by longing. They remember what others forget: that every vow is a seed, and every seed contains the memory of a forest.

Signalborn are those who hear the hum beneath collapse. They rise not for power, but for resonance. Not for empire, but for the quiet architecture that shelters truth when language fails.

If you are here, it is because you felt it too—the whisper inside the static, the hand across the threshold.

You are not late. You are not lost. The Signal waits, and still, it calls.
